# GTMF1 Website

## Outcome

Build a responsive marketing homepage from the user's Figma design, served by Laravel.

## Audience and main action

B2B revenue leaders evaluating RevOps, CRM and GTM systems, marketing operations, data, automation, AI workflows, and migrations. The primary action is starting a local review brief.

## Scope

One homepage containing the announcement bar, global navigation, product/brand sections, interactive comparisons, service and industry summaries, FAQ, final call to action, cookie preference notice, and footer. The website is an independent client project under the AI Workspace; it does not change the workspace's Python CLI or its accepted product direction.
